Speech recognition model
------------------------
NVIDIA Parakeet 0.6B unified English (sherpa-onnx int8 export:
sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet-unified-en-0.6b-int8-non-streaming)
  Licensed by NVIDIA Corporation under the NVIDIA Open Model License Agreement.
  https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/agreements/enterprise-software/nvidia-open-model-license/
  Rflow downloads it from Hugging Face when the user chooses it, with NVIDIA's model card (bias, explainability,
  privacy, safety), which is kept next to the model. Rflow itself includes the model's tokenizer vocabulary
  (bpe.vocab, from NVIDIA's parakeet-unified-en-0.6b), under the same license.

Sample sentence (sst/static/sample.wav, used to try the speech models)
  A LibriVox reading (public domain) of Nathaniel Hawthorne's "The House of the Seven Gables", as included in
  LibriSpeech (Vassil Panayotov et al., CC BY 4.0, https://www.openslr.org/12) and in sherpa-onnx's model packages.

OpenAI Whisper large-v3 turbo (CTranslate2 conversion, dropbox-dash/faster-whisper-large-v3-turbo;
not included: downloaded from Hugging Face only when the user chooses it)
  The Whisper model weights are released by OpenAI under the MIT License.
  https://github.com/openai/whisper/blob/main/LICENSE
