How it works

Three steps, and you're dictating.

Install

Run Rflow-Setup.exe. No admin rights needed; the speech model is included, so it also works offline.

Click in a text box

Any app: Teams, Slack, Outlook, Word, VS Code, your browser...

Hold Ctrl+Win and speak

Your key, your microphone, your words

Pick the microphone, the dictation key and whether to clean up the text. For the cleanup, enter any OpenAI-compatible endpoint and choose a model from its list, with a backup model in case it fails. Add the names and terms you use, so they come out right.

If the endpoint is slow or unreachable, Rflow simply types what it heard, so dictation never waits.

Questions

Is my voice sent anywhere?

No. Speech is recognised on your laptop by NVIDIA Parakeet. Only if you switch on text cleanup is the finished text (never audio) sent to the endpoint you entered.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

The installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

What does it need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a microphone and about 800 MB of disk space. No GPU, no internet connection (except for the optional cleanup and for updates).

Which languages?

English, for now.

How do updates work?

Rflow checks for a new version when it starts and every few hours. You'll see a banner in its window; click Update now. The download is checked against its published checksum before it's installed, and Rflow restarts itself.

Nothing is typed in one particular app

That app probably runs as administrator; Windows doesn't let normal programs type into those.
