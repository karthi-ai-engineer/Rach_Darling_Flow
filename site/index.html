Dictation that stays out of the way

A small app with one job: turn what you say into text, wherever you are typing.

⌨️

Works in every app

Chat, email, documents, code editors, the browser: the text goes where your cursor is. Your clipboard is left as it was.

🔒

Recognised on your laptop

NVIDIA Parakeet turns speech into text on your own computer, so your voice never leaves it. Or choose Whisper for 99 languages, a cloud model with your own key, or your own server.

🎙️

Hold, or go hands-free

Hold Ctrl+Win while you talk, or tap it (or Ctrl+Win+Space) to keep recording. Esc cancels.

🔁

Transform it afterwards

Select text, or just finish a dictation, and press Ctrl+Alt+T: make it concise, professional, bullet points or action items. Numbers, names and your "maybe" stay; nothing is invented.

✨

Optionally let an AI model you choose (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, or Ollama and vLLM on your own server) add punctuation, drop "um"s and spell your terms right.

A small pill at the bottom of the screen: a live level while you speak, then "Typed". It never steals focus.

⬆️

Updates itself

How it works

Three steps, and you're dictating.

Install

Run Rflow-Setup.exe. No admin rights needed. Rflow then helps you choose how it recognises your speech: download Parakeet once (about 660 MB) and it works offline, or use a cloud model or your own server. Then pick your microphone.

Click in a text box

Any app: Teams, Slack, Outlook, Word, VS Code, your browser...

Hold Ctrl+Win and speak

Let go when you're done. The text appears where your cursor is, usually in well under a second.

A real app, not just a tray icon

Open Rflow from the Start menu, or click its icon near the clock. The first time, it helps you choose how it recognises your speech and your microphone, and try your first dictation. After that, everything is in one window:

Home

Your recent dictations, ready to copy again, and your words per minute.

Dictionary

Names and terms Rflow should spell your way.

Speech recognition

Parakeet, Whisper, a cloud model or your own server. "Scan my computer" tells you which fits.

AI cleanup

Your provider, key and model, with a Test button.

Reading test

Read 30 sentences and see how well Rflow understands you.

Settings and profiles

Your key and microphone, and one setup per person sharing the computer.

Closing the window keeps Rflow running in the background, so dictation still works everywhere.

Your key, your microphone, your words

Pick the microphone, the dictation key and whether to clean up the text. For the cleanup, choose your AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, or Ollama, vLLM or any OpenAI-compatible server of your own), paste its key and pick a model from its list, with a backup model in case it fails. Add the names and terms you use, so they come out right.

If the provider is slow or unreachable, Rflow simply types what it heard, so dictation never waits. Sharing the computer? Each person gets a profile with their own key, words, provider and history.

Questions

Is my voice sent anywhere?

Not with Parakeet or Whisper: they recognise speech on your laptop. If you choose a cloud model (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq), each recording goes to that provider, and Rflow asks you first; with your own server, it goes to that server. AI cleanup, if you switch it on, sends only the finished text (never audio) to the provider you chose. Your API keys are encrypted for your Windows account.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

The installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

What does it need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a microphone and about 1 GB of disk space: about 300 MB for Rflow and 660 MB for Parakeet. No GPU. The internet is needed once, to download Parakeet; after that only for a cloud model, the optional cleanup and updates.

Which languages?

English with Parakeet. Whisper large-v3 turbo (a 1.6 GB download, quick with an NVIDIA graphics card) and the cloud models know many more, Tamil and Japanese among them.

How do updates work?

Rflow checks for a new version when it starts and every few hours. You'll see a banner in its window; click Update now. The download is checked against its published checksum before it's installed, and Rflow restarts itself.

Nothing is typed in one particular app

That app probably runs as administrator; Windows doesn't let normal programs type into those.
